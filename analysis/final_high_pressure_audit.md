# Final high-pressure simulation response audit

The corrected high-pressure CFD archive is the numerical source for these results. This audit records 24 geometrically defined CFD cases (12 H2 and 12 N2): 23 contain pressure and force histories through solver iteration 3000, while the corrected nitrogen 5 m/s, obturator A case ends at iteration 1372. The nominal study state in the archive is 437 bar and 233 K; the actual boundary condition is a zero *kinematic-pressure reference*, and the fluid state is represented through the constant-density and viscosity inputs.

**Gas properties from active dictionaries**: H2 nu=2.709e-7 m2/s, rhoInf=33.77 kg/m3; N2 nu=7.932e-8 m2/s, rhoInf=481.352 kg/m3.

**Processing**: for each case, match output values by solver iteration and compute the arithmetic mean and population CV over the final 301 iterations. The interval is 2700–3000 for 23 cases and 1072–1372 for nitrogen 5 m/s obturator A. Delta-p [Pa] = rhoInf*(P_in-P_out), where P is an area-averaged kinematic pressure; F_x [N] is the sum of the recorded dimensional pressure and viscous force x-components and must not be multiplied by rhoInf again. The CSV retains pressure in kPa and axial force in N. CV is a descriptive terminal-window screening statistic, not proof of convergence.

**Corrected source data**: The revised nitrogen 5 m/s obturator-A case now has numerical histories distinct from hydrogen, with a valid nitrogen reference density (481.352 kg/m³) and kinematic viscosity (7.932e-8 m²/s). Over its last 301 pressure/force samples (iterations 1072–1372), mean pressure loss is 471.7202 kPa and mean axial force is 79.46193 N; population CV values are 0.759% and 0.723%, respectively. The pressure and force outputs end at iteration 1372, and the last yPlus record is at iteration 1300 (wallObt maximum yPlus 1449.706). This *shorter-run* case is now included in thesis tables but is explicitly tagged SCREENED_SHORTER_RUN in the analysis CSV. These indicators describe terminal stability and do not establish full solver convergence.

**Numerical-quality cautions**: Hydrogen obturator B at 10 and 15 m/s has substantial terminal variation; hydrogen B at 15 m/s has pressure CV 10.246% and force CV 12.461%. Maximum obturator yPlus ranges from about 156 to 1256 for H2 and reaches approximately 4306 for N2. The highest N2 delta-p is about 4.225 MPa (9.7% of 43.7 MPa). The archive contains no comprehensive solver residual logs or grid-sensitivity reports in the inspected case files. These data support descriptive CFD comparison, not validated valve closure or certification.

H2 is the principal refuelling-gas study. N2 is a supplementary industrial-fluid comparison at equal inlet velocity; its substantially higher density means the flow rates are not comparable on an equal-mass-flow basis.

**Reconciliation against the revised archive**: The other 23 gas/velocity/geometry combinations were re-parsed from their pressure and force outputs and agree with the numerical values already reported. The correction changes only the N2 / 5 m/s / obturator A response and associated status/iteration metadata. Hydrogen remains the primary engineering study.
